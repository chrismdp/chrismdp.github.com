# Newsletter and social context

The newsletter and LinkedIn now live outside this repo. `/newsletter` and `/linkedin` own the
current workflow, and drafts live in the vault under `content/newsletters/` and
`content/posts/`. The notes below were recorded in this repo and are kept for the background
they carry.

## Newsletter

Newsletter content goes out through Kit, following a rough template of one story, one idea,
and one question each week.

**Style**: less formal and more intimate than a blog post, with a more enthusiastic tone. Do
not make anything up. Only use what is known to be true. Ask Chris for stories when you need
them.

**Audience**: `/newsletter` owns this. See `references/angle-and-voice.md` in that skill.

**Linking**: when a newsletter links to a blog post, use the full absolute URL, such as
`https://www.chrismdp.com/coding-with-ai/`, so the link works in an email client.

## Social media posts

`/linkedin` owns formatting, hooks, mention syntax, and where drafts live.
