# Newsletter and social context

The newsletter and LinkedIn now live outside this repo. `/newsletter` and `/linkedin` own the current workflow, and drafts live in the vault under `content/newsletters/` and `content/posts/`. The notes below were recorded in this repo and are kept for the background they carry.

## Newsletter

Newsletter content goes out through Kit, following a rough template of one story, one idea, and one question each week.

**Style**: less formal and more intimate than a blog post, with a more enthusiastic tone. Do not make anything up. Only use what is known to be true. Ask Chris for stories when you need them.

**Context**: people expect content about using AI to build agents and products at high speed, generating revenue quickly, with weekly notes sharing stories, learnings, and tips on getting ahead with AI.

**Linking**: when a newsletter links to a blog post, use the full absolute URL, such as `https://www.chrismdp.com/coding-with-ai/`, so the link works in an email client.

## Social media posts

- No markdown bold. LinkedIn shows literal asterisks.
- No emoji headers. Use simple flowing paragraphs.
- LinkedIn mentions use `@` names, such as "@Chris Parsons" and "@AI In Action By Chris Parsons".
- Store drafts in the `_drafts/` folder.
